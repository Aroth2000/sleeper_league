- **QB:** Kyler Murray released; Jacoby Brissett (re-signed after brief holdout) is Week 1 starter. Gardner Minshew II backup; 3rd-rd rookie Carson Beck (Miami) stashed for future, flashing but turnover-prone.
- **Backfield:** Not a clean bell cow — rookie Jeremiyah Love (No. 3 overall) has emerged atop the depth chart over James Conner (foot recovery) and Tyler Allgeier, though OC Nathaniel Hackett won't name a starter. Trey Benson has fallen to RB4/roster bubble.
- **Injuries/PUP:** OLB Josh Sweat, CB Garrett Williams, TE Tip Reiman (ankle), LT Paris Johnson Jr. (knee, "maintenance") opened on PUP. Benson has a knee flare-up.
- **WR:** Open WR1 battle — Michael Wilson trending ahead of Marvin Harrison Jr. (drop issues, though rebounding). Zay Jones has "decisively won" WR3 over Greg Dortch. Rookie 5th-rounder Reggie Virgil flashing as a role-earner.
- **TE:** Trey McBride (record $76M extension) is the unquestioned focal point.
- **New regime:** HC Gannon fired; Mike LaFleur hired HC, Nathaniel Hackett calling plays — motion-heavy, multi-TE scheme.

### Atlanta Falcons
- **QB:** New HC Kevin Stefanski/OC Tommy Rees. Tua Tagovailoa (signed FA) projected Week 1 starter over Michael Penix Jr., who's "highly unlikely" ready after Nov. 2025 ACL surgery (limited to individual/7-on-7 work).

- **WR:** Drake London is the locked-in WR1. Darnell Mooney released; Jahan Dotson and Olamide Zaccheaus signed to fight for WR2 alongside returning depth. Rookie Zachariah Branch (3rd rd) drawing buzz, secured return role, offensive role still developing.
