## Jacksonville Jaguars Offense — 2026 Training Camp/Offseason (verified via live web search, not Wikipedia)

**Big picture:** The 2025 Jaguars leading rusher, Travis Etienne Jr., left in free agency (signed with New Orleans, March 11, 2026, after 5 seasons in Jax). That reshaped the backfield, and this camp is being run under HC/playcaller Liam Coen entering Year 2, with a rebuilt WR room around a big-money trade acquisition (Jakobi Meyers) and a two-way star (Travis Hunter) working his way back from injury.

**QB:** No real "battle" — Trevor Lawrence is the unquestioned starter and is reportedly playing with more confidence/calm in Year 2 under Coen, with strong camp reps (5 TDs in one practice, connecting on 20+ yard throws to five different receivers). Nick Mullens is the clear backup, running second-team/red-zone reps. Carter Bradley and UDFA Joey Aguilar are competing for a practice-squad spot behind him — not a real QB2 battle.

**RB — no bell cow, committee reshuffled by Etienne's exit:**
- Etienne signed with the Saints in free agency (3/11/26) after leading Jax in rushing in 2025 (1,107 yds, 7 TD).
- The Jags' first outside free-agent signing of the 2026 offseason was RB Chris Rodriguez Jr. (ex-Commanders, Liam Coen coached him at Kentucky), and per depth-chart reporting he's currently penciled in atop the RB group.
- Rodriguez has been managing a knee injury in camp (missed early team reps doing individual work, then had his "most active" day around Day 7, described as excelling in goal-line/short-yardage work); he also took a "maintenance day" on Day 6.
- Bhayshul Tuten (2025 rookie) is pushing hard for early-down/receiving work and broke a long run in camp.
- LeQuint Allen Jr. is the passing-down back; Ameer Abdullah and DeeJay Dallas/J'Mari Taylor/Ja'Quinden Jackson round out the room fighting for the last spots.
- Note: earlier in 2025, Tank Bigsby (once part of the RB mix) was traded to the Eagles (Sept. 8, 2025) for 2026 5th/6th-round picks after being buried behind Etienne and Tuten — he's no longer part of this year's picture.
- Bottom line: this remains a committee, not a bell-cow situation, and it's a notably different committee than last year since Etienne is gone.

**WR — set at the top, real battle for the back end:**
- Locked-in top three/four: Brian Thomas Jr. (alpha, drawing praise for contested/TD catches), Jakobi Meyers (acquired via trade from the Raiders, then signed a 3-year/$60M extension — a major offseason move that elevated the room), and Parker Washington (also the punt returner). Travis Hunter is also taking real WR snaps in addition to cornerback.
- Hunter's status is a notable injury story: he missed the final 10 games of 2025 with a knee injury, spent 9 months rehabbing, but has fully participated since Day 1 of 2026 camp, reportedly added 10 lbs of muscle and clocked 22.6 mph (aiming for 23). He'll keep playing both WR and CB.
- Rookie sixth-rounder Josh Cameron has been one of camp's biggest risers once healthy — deep, contested catches and drawing multiple pass-interference calls; Coen has praised his physicality at 223-225 lbs.
- Rookie CJ Williams has "consistently impressed" and looks like he's clinched a spot in the top-six WR rotation (including a diving, full-extension TD grab), pushing for real snaps behind the three established starters.
- Austin Trammell is depth/special-teams-type competing for a roster spot.

**TE — youth movement, one starter but two rookies pushing for real snaps:**
- Brenton Strange is the clear TE1 (he'd shown strong 2025 form — 20 catches/204 yds in 4 games — before a hip injury sent him to IR for a stretch last season; healthy now and anchoring the room).
- The real camp story is a rookie battle behind him: 2026 rookie Tanner Koziol has been a standout, earning real offensive snaps beyond a typical TE2 role (highlight catch + TD in camp), while Nate Boerkircher — the presumptive No. 2 TE — is now sharing more reps with Koziol than expected. Quintin Morris is veteran depth.

**Injuries/PUP list:** LT Cole Van Lanen (knee) and OL Garrett DiGiorgio (foot) opened/have remained on the PUP list through camp. RB Chris Rodriguez Jr. has been dealing with a knee issue in camp (not on PUP, working through it). On the O-line, the Jaguars otherwise retained all nine returning linemen and added rookie Emmanuel Pregnon (competing as Ezra Cleveland's backup at guard); Walker Little has been cross-training and emerging as a right guard option, and Anton Harrison continues at left tackle. Injuries flagged elsewhere on the roster (defense) don't affect the offense.

**Trades/free agency recap (offense):**
- Traded away: Tank Bigsby to Philadelphia (Sept. 2025, for 2026 5th/6th picks).
- Lost in free agency: Travis Etienne Jr. to New Orleans (March 2026).
- Acquired via trade + re-signed: WR Jakobi Meyers from the Raiders, then extended 3 years/$60M.
- Signed in free agency: RB Chris Rodriguez Jr. (Jaguars' first outside FA move of the 2026 offseason).

**Rookies earning real roles (offense):** WR CJ Williams and WR Josh Cameron (both look like true rotation/contributor pieces, not just camp bodies), and TE Tanner Koziol (outproducing his expected TE2/3 role and cutting into Nate Boerkircher's snaps). OL Emmanuel Pregnon is developing as first guard depth.
