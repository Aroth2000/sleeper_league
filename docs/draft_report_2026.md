# 2026 Draft Report — Sunday Scaries

Full post-draft breakdown, built from the actual completed Sleeper draft (all 150 picks pulled live). One correction to note up front: the draft ran **15 rounds, not 16** as the pre-draft docs assumed — which resolves the "bench count unconfirmed" flag from `draft_board_2026.md`. 9 starters (QB/RB/RB/WR/WR/TE/FLEX/SUPER_FLEX/DEF) + **6 bench = 15**, exactly. That arithmetic mismatch we flagged pre-draft is now closed out.

## Biggest surprise vs. the pre-draft board

**Puka Nacua went 3rd overall** (to Pabst Interference/tlekes), not late-first as our board projected. That's a real signal tlekes either isn't worried about the suspension risk we flagged or is willing to eat it for his talent floor — worth watching if a suspension does land, since that roster would suddenly have a real hole. The silver lining for Andrew: with Nacua gone by pick 3, **Christian McCaffrey fell all the way to pick 8**, which turned out to be a big win (more below).

Two data oddities worth flagging rather than ignoring: Sleeper's live data now lists **Jaylen Waddle under Denver** and **Kyler Murray under Minnesota** (both drafted by LoochCarluccio) — that doesn't match anything in our prior research, so it's either a very recent, unreported transaction or a data quirk on Sleeper's end. Worth a sanity check before you assume either player suits up for those teams in Week 1.

## League-wide themes

**TE is badly bifurcated.** Four rosters doubled up on legitimately good tight ends (jpalmeri1616: Kelce+Andrews; DannyBC1: McBride+Kittle; PeterCrisileo: Bowers+Likely; havicht: Loveland+Henry), while five rosters — including yours — are running a single starter with zero insurance (Andrew, pdustin, jomud) or two unproven names with real bust risk (Edeecher). If you're looking for a trade angle, the TE-rich rosters have real surplus to deal from.

**RB scarcity played out roughly as the pre-draft research predicted.** Andrew, PeterCrisileo, Edeecher, and LoochCarluccio all walked away with a true workhorse-caliber back (or two, in Andrew's case), while tlekes and jomud are running committee-only RB rooms with no clear bell-cow — those two are the likeliest early-season RB streamers/waiver hunters.

**Superflex QB depth split the league cleanly.** jpalmeri1616 (Lamar+Mahomes), tlekes (Hurts+Goff+Stroud), and Edeecher/DannyBC1/jomud all have two-plus real starters. havicht is the outlier with real exposure — only Stafford is a lock, and that's worth watching if you're looking for a trade partner desperate for QB depth.

**One clean, actionable miss:** LoochCarluccio drafted zero defense. That's a free roster edge for whoever's fastest on the waiver wire once the season opens.

## Postscript: what Andrew could have done better

Looking at the actual pick-by-pick data, two real, provable misses stand out — plus one structural tradeoff worth thinking about for next year.

**The clearest miss: pick 28 (Round 3).** Andrew took George Pickens — but Malik Nabers was still on the board, and LoochCarluccio grabbed him with the very next pick (29). Nabers graded out Tier 1 (true elite target-monster) while Pickens graded mid-Tier 2. A straightforward "should have taken the better player who was right there."

**The bigger structural question:** Andrew paired three elite RBs (CMC, Saquon, Kenneth Walker) but only two RB slots plus a shared FLEX can start them. That third stud back is a nice trade chip, but isn't lineup value the way a third elite piece at a position you can actually start would be. Meanwhile, the first QB pick didn't come until round 8 (Sam Darnold) — by then Lamar, Mahomes, Herbert, Burrow, Hurts, Allen, and Stroud were all long gone. In a Superflex league two QBs start every week, and Darnold/Nix (both solid, neither special) is a real value gap next to jpalmeri1616's Lamar+Mahomes or DannyBC1's Herbert. Justin Herbert was still available at pick 13 and went to DannyBC1 at pick 26 — 13 picks later.

**A related, cheaper fix:** two late-round picks doubled and tripled up on RB depth that wasn't needed — Jadarian Price at pick 68 and Jordan James at pick 113 — while single-TE risk (flagged above) went unaddressed. Tucker Kraft was still on the board at pick 68 (gone one pick later, to LoochCarluccio), and both Hunter Henry and Mark Andrews were still available at pick 113. Either pick could have gone to real TE2 insurance behind Sam LaPorta instead.

None of this changes the big picture — Andrew's RB room is still the best in the league by a wide margin, and Darnold/Nix are perfectly startable, just not special. The corrections are mostly about where the marginal value went: one clean whiff (Nabers), and a pattern of over-investing in a position already solved at the expense of one that wasn't (QB depth, TE insurance).

## League power ranking

Full completeness/floor-ceiling ranking, most to least complete roster:

1. **DannyBC1** — no real hole: elite WR1 (Amon-Ra), strong ascending RB1 (Kyren), legit QB1 (Herbert), and a double-elite TE room (McBride + Kittle) nobody else matched.
2. **Big Mommy Milkers (jpalmeri1616)** — the best QB pairing in the league (Lamar + Mahomes) and a strong TE duo (Kelce + Andrews); only kept off the top spot by a slightly softer RB room.
3. **Still at RPI (PeterCrisileo)** — Jonathan Taylor + Brock Bowers is as safe an elite foundation as exists in this draft; QB (Lawrence/Maye) is the one place it's not elite.
4. **Ethan's Younglings (Edeecher)** — Gibbs + Hampton is a fantastic RB1/RB2 pair and Daniels is a real QB1, but WR/TE depth is speculative — high ceiling, wider range of outcomes than the teams above it.
5. **Andrew (you)** — the best three-man RB room in the league by a wide margin, two startable QBs, but no true alpha WR and zero TE insurance behind LaPorta keeps this from cracking the top tier.
6. **jomud** — CeeDee Lamb + Joe Burrow is a strong top two and the WR corps fits the scoring format well, but the RB room has no workhorse and TE is a single unproven rookie.
7. **Mass General Hospital (pdustin)** — the safest possible QB duo (Allen + Purdy) and good depth everywhere, but nothing at WR rises above "very good," and TE is single-deep.
8. **Pabst Interference (tlekes)** — Puka Nacua's talent and three good QBs are real assets, but only three rostered RBs and none with a clean workhorse role is a real problem given how scarce RB is league-wide, plus the Nacua suspension risk hanging over the roster's best pick.
9. **Water, Barkley, and Hops (havicht)** — Ja'Marr Chase and a strong TE room are great, but Matthew Stafford is the only reliable QB on the roster in a league where two starting lineup spots need one every week — the most fragile structural situation in the league, and Jeanty's ankle adds a second live flag on top of it.
10. **Glizzy Guzzler (LoochCarluccio)** — ironically has arguably the best top-three picks in the entire draft (Bijan, Jefferson, Nabers), but finished with zero rostered defense, an unforced gap nobody else has to deal with.

Worth remembering this is a draft-day snapshot, not a season projection — injuries (Jeanty), legal outcomes (Nacua, Jacobs), and one waiver claim (LoochCarluccio's DEF) could all reshuffle this within a few weeks.
